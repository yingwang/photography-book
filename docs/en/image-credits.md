# Image Credits and Reuse Guidelines

> This page traces the images used in the text to specific artwork or collection records, identifying local copies in the repository by dimensions and SHA-256 checksums. Early commits did not preserve the original download command for each image; therefore, the "artwork record" does not necessarily correspond to the original download URL. If you intend to reuse or republish any image, please verify the latest rights statements on the linked pages, the terms governing digital reproductions, and the applicable laws in your jurisdiction. This page does not constitute legal advice, nor should an earlier creation date serve as a substitute for item-by-item verification.
